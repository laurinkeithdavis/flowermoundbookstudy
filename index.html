Study Format & Scope

Intensive Big Book Reading

Together, we will read line-by-line through the foundational text of Alcoholics Anonymous, starting from the Preface up to page 164.

Format Note: We cover all text from the Preface through page 164, except for two chapters: “To the Wives” and “To the Employers”.

Small Group Focus

Shared reading, reflection, and community building in a secluded lake retreat setting.

What to Bring & Lodging

Please review accommodation and meal details carefully before arriving.

1

Your Big Book

Bring your personal Alcoholics Anonymous Big Book (4th Edition recommended) along with highlighters or pens for taking notes during study sessions.

2

Sleeping & Air Mattresses

There are beds available for 12–13 participants (some shared beds).

Bed Assignments: Based on length of sobriety & at the discretion of organizers 😊

Bring an air mattress if you need a bed beyond availability or prefer not to share a bed.

3

Meals & Dietary Needs

We will prepare meals together as a group! 5 full meals and snacks are provided.

  • Simple meals (sandwiches, pizza) during study hours
  • Saturday Night: Steak Dinner
  • Sunday Morning: Waffles Breakfast
  • Please bring your own personal snacks or special dietary items.
